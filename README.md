Privacy Policy for Discord Bot
1. Data We Collect
To provide its core functionality, this Discord bot may collect and process certain limited technical and user data. This includes, but is not limited to: User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, Channel IDs, timestamp logs, and any user-provided commands or configuration choices. We do not store full message contents unless strictly required for a specific command feature.
2. How We Use Your Data
The data collected is used solely to maintain, operate, and optimize the performance of the bot within your servers. We use this information to track custom commands, handle configuration preferences, prevent abuse or service disruptions, and improve overall bot stability.
3. Data Retention and Deletion
We only retain your data for as long as your server continues to use the bot or as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes outlined in this policy. If you wish to have your data manually deleted from our storage, you may contact the bot owner or remove the bot from your server entirely, which may automatically flush cached configurations depending on system architecture.
4. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information or server data to outside third parties. Your data remains strictly restricted to the operational backend hosting environment of the bot.
5. User Rights and Agreement
By adding this bot to your server or interacting with its features, you explicitly agree to the data collection and usage practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please remove the bot from your server.
